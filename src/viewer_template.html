How it is made

Photometry

LM-63 (IES) files drive everything. The reader handles all symmetry types, so manufacturer files drop straight in. This demo uses generic distributions.

Calculation

Point-by-point direct light, plus radiosity interreflection with exact form factors ( patches, all bounces).

Scenes

Light is linear in each circuit's dimming, so the Dinner and Calm levels are solved by bounded least squares against zone targets.

Renders

Blender Cycles uses the same IES files. A calibration render fixes the watts-per-candela factor, so the linear passes give real cd/m² and lx.

Sensory Calm

The 40–80 lx band comes from the author's MSc field study of light and indoor well-being of autistic adults (DTU, 2025).

Limits

The empty-room calculation leaves out furniture, and there are no glare or melanopic metrics yet. Treat it as a design study, not a compliance certificate.
